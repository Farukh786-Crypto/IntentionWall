Shalom Salam

Peace in Hebrew. Peace in Arabic. The same word, twice.

Two young entrepreneurs in Los Angeles, cut off from their families by a war neither of them started, open a hummus stand. Customers begin sending a prayer along with their order. The prayers are captured, transcribed, and carried the whole way — folded into the Western Wall, read aloud in the mosque, sent on to the church.

They started with one physical location. Then they took it global. The film is in post-production and entering festivals; when the credits roll, a QR code puts the wall in the audience’s hands.

One of our goals is to make hope and understanding and peace viral.

Daniel & Bayou - Dream Team Directors

A film, an idea and a Wall

Three steps, one greater story. Connected by a belief in a kinder world.

01

The Film

Two people from different backgrounds sat down over hummus and discovered something powerful: despite their differences, they shared the same hopes for a better tomorrow.

02

The Idea

Their conversations revealed a simple truth: small acts of empathy can cross borders, challenge stereotypes and bring people closer. The film captures these moments, real people, real perspectives, real hope.

03

The Wall

That idea grew into an intention wall, a global space where anyone, anywhere can share their intentions for peace. What started as a conversation is now a living wall of hope, written by people around the world.
